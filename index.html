Who we are

Engineering intelligence into hardware.

IABEX Technologies is a Pune-based electronics engineering company delivering end-to-end solutions for industrial and commercial customers. We develop our own embedded products and build fully customised electronics for our clients.

We specialise in special purpose machines, embedded systems, IoT and automation — with deep expertise in PCB design, STM32 and ARM platforms and industrial-grade enclosures. We build it right, the first time.

How we work

From whiteboard to working hardware.

No hand-offs between vendors, no gaps between design intent and what ships.

  1. STEP 01
    Requirement & concept

    We study the application, environment and cost target, then propose an architecture.

  2. STEP 02
    Design

    Schematic capture, PCB layout, firmware and enclosure selection — in-house.

  3. STEP 03
    Prototype & validate

    Working prototypes tested on the bench and in the field until they meet spec.

  4. STEP 04
    Produce & support

    Assembly, testing and delivery at volume, backed by repair and long-term support.
